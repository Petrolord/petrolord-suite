Limits of this analysis
- Black-oil model as the deck defines it (OIL, WATER, GAS, DISGAS): no compositional or thermal effects.
- Grid resolution: 300 cells of 500 by 500 ft. Results are averages over each cell; gradients finer than a cell (coning, near-well saturation fronts) are
not resolved.
- The builder grid is a layer-cake box: flat layers, no faults, no corner-point geometry.
- PVT: Entered in the Model Builder: black-oil correlations of Fluid Systems Studio (Standing Pb, Rs and Bo; Beggs-Robinson oil viscosity;
Dranchuk-Abou-Kassem Z) from the typed API, gas gravity, temperature and GOR; PVTW typed.
- Saturation functions: Corey parameters entered in the Model Builder; no capillary pressure. Two-phase tables; the three-phase oil relative
permeability is the simulator's default model; no hysteresis and no end-point scaling unless the deck says so.
- No aquifer is modelled in the main deck: pressure support comes from the wells only.
- Wells are controlled as the deck declares; no well economic limits or group controls are applied unless the deck holds them.
- A single deterministic run: no uncertainty range. The material balance and convergence statements are the simulator's own printout as read by
the worker.

Flags on this run
No flag: the balance closes, no step was cut and the inputs are traced.
