# Ekene Demonstration Dataset — Plan of Record

Status: **APPROVED, owner decisions 2026-09-11.** Built for the YouTube
application tutorial series (ten episode scripts drafted 2026-09-10).
Waves run in order, one PR per wave, generator committed to the Suite
repo, built artifacts published to a GitHub release.

## 1. What this is

The ten tutorial scripts were written without naming a single file. Every
one of them says some version of "use whatever data you have", and the
Well Data Manager pilot adds the instruction that carries the series:

> Keep the same well throughout, because every later section builds on it.

The scripts were therefore already written for one carried dataset. It
did not exist. This program builds it.

The scripts run as one chain, and the chain is the product argument:

| # | Episode | Consumes | Produces |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Well Data Manager | LAS, survey, tops, checkshots | the registry well |
| 2 | Petrophysics Studio | the registry well | Vsh, PHIT, PHIE, Sw, K, pay; zones |
| 3 | Well Correlation | wells + tops | a saved section, corrected tops |
| 4 | Mapping & Surface Studio | tops across wells | TOP_SAND and BASE_SAND surfaces |
| 5 | ReservoirCalc Pro | a surface + a zone + a polygon | STOIIP, deterministic and probabilistic |
| 6 | Seismolord | SEG-Y + wells with sonic/density/checkshots | a horizon, a tie, a time-depth surface |
| 7 | Earth Modeling | surfaces from 4 and 6, zones from 2 | a layered model, volumes |
| 8 | Stratigraphy Studio | the saved section, dated tops, a lithology log | a Wheeler chart, a net sand map |
| 9 | Pore Pressure Studio | sonic + density + calibration points | pore pressure, fracture gradient |
| 10 | Well Design Studio | the map, the prognosis, offset surveys | a trajectory with an error model |

Integration is the thing being demonstrated, so the dataset has to be
*one* field, and the numbers have to agree wherever two applications look
at the same rock.

## 2. Owner decisions (2026-09-11)

1. **Extend Ekene.** Not a new field, not a public real dataset. The
   videos, the Academy and the engine goldens describe one field.
2. **Hybrid: synthetic field, real seismic character.** Every value is
   generated from the Ekene spine; the seismic wavelet, bandwidth, noise
   floor and attenuation are calibrated to look like field data.
3. **Additive extension only.** A deeper second reservoir and six more
   wells. No locked number changes; NextGen and the engine suite stay
   green.
4. **GitHub Releases** on the Petrolord org for the heavy artifacts. The
   generator lives in this repo; the release is versioned and a video can
   name the release it was recorded against.

## 3. What already exists (audit 2026-09-11)

`packages/engines/test-data/ekene-dynamic/` holds the Ekene teaching
field, generated by `packages/engines/tools/ekene-dynamic/generate.mjs`:
six wells with map coordinates, top and base sand and roles; OWC 1560 m;
STOIIP 12.139208107496763 MMstb; PVT (API 32, gas SG 0.75, 35,000 ppm,
Rsi 400 scf/stb, Pi 3200 psia, Pb 2000 psia, 180 °F); Corey relative
permeability, a Leverett-J drainage curve, k 250 md, phi 0.20, NTG 0.80,
Swi 0.35; monthly production 2020-01 to 2025-12 with a waterflood from
2023-01. Ten NextGen courses teach off it.

**The structure is reproducible.** The locked NG5 volumetrics are not a
free-standing table: they are what you get by gridding the six wells'
TOP_SAND picks. Verified 2026-09-11:

```
topsToPoints(WELLS,'TOP_SAND') -> specForPoints(pts,100,2) ->
gridSurface(pts,spec,{maxExtrapolation:800})
  spec  x0 400, y0 800, dx 100, dy 100, nx 25, ny 20
  live  201 nodes
  cells above the 1560 m contact   169          (NG5 golden: 169)
  max oil column                   20.2818603515625 m
                                   (NG5 golden: 20.2818603515625)
```

So any well that adds a TOP_SAND control point moves the volumetrics.
That constraint shapes §4.

What the spine has **no** data for is exactly what the ten episodes need:
logs, deviation surveys, checkshots, seismic, pore pressure, well
designs. All of it is derivable from the spine, and deriving it rather
than inventing it is what makes the series hold together.

## 4. Field design

### 4.1 Frame

- **CRS** WGS 84 / UTM zone 32N (EPSG:32632). Survey origin E 400000,
  N 520000; the locked local coordinates are offsets from it. The origin
  is a multiple of the 100 m cell, so the gridded surface is a pure
  translation and the volumetrics above reproduce bit for bit.
- **Offshore**, shallow water. Water depth 35 m, KB 25 m above MSL,
  mudline 60 m MD. Ekene-1 is the discovery well and the series hero.
- **Depths.** The locked numbers are MD below KB, and the six development
  wells are vertical, so MD = TVD. TVDSS = MD − 25.
- **Surfaces are elevation, negative down** (the MS5 owner rule): the
  TOP_SAND surface stores −1548 where the well posts 1548 m.

### 4.2 Stratigraphy (Ekene-1, m MD below KB)

| Depth | Surface | Age | Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| 60 | Seabed | 0 | — |
| 1150 | Benin / Agbada | 5.3 Ma | formation boundary |
| 1290 | Ogbia Shale | 12.5 Ma | maximum flooding surface; top of the pressure ramp |
| 1548 | **Ekene Sand** (locked, per well) | 14.8 Ma | sequence boundary |
| 1580 | Ekene Sand base (locked, per well) | — | — |
| 1790 | **Oboro Unconformity** | hiatus 16.4 → 20.5 Ma | subaerial unconformity |
| 1845 | **Oboro Sand** | 21.0 Ma | sequence boundary |
| 1912 | Oboro Sand base | — | — |
| 2080 | Akata Formation | 28.0 Ma | formation boundary |
| 2250 | TD | — | — |

The Oboro Unconformity carries the hiatus the Stratigraphy episode needs
for its Wheeler chart. Ekene Sand is oil (OWC 1560 m); **Oboro Sand is
gas** (GWC 1935 m), which gives the Petrophysics episode its real
density-neutron gas crossover and the field a second target.

Added (additive; every TOP_SAND pick below except Ekene-7's is **sampled
from the structural model**, which is exact at all seven locked picks —
see §4.5. The volumetric shift from adding them is asserted and reported
by the generator: 171 oil cells and a 19.24 m column against the locked
six-well 169 and 20.28, the difference being Ekene-7's real blind

| Well | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Ekene-7 | vertical appraisal at (1500, 1500), TOP_SAND **1549** — the locked NG7 blind-test value, 5.67 m deeper than the six-well grid predicts. Episode 4's re-grid beat is that blind test. |
| Ekene-8 | deviated Oboro producer from the platform; full suite incl. PEF |
| Ekene-9 | deviated Oboro producer; **GR, DT, RT only — no RHOB**, so Episode 9's Gardner-substitution beat has a real well to point at |
| Ekene-10 | deviated Oboro producer; carries the **lithology log** and core photographs for Episode 8 |
| Ekene-11 | the **planned** well of Episode 10. Plan only, no logs. |
| Ekene-12 | second pad wellbore, surveys only — the anti-collision neighbour |

The fault at x = 1800 already exists in the spine's doctrine (the DC22
sealing-fault what-if). Put in the seismic as a growth fault — throw 8 m
at Ekene Sand, 45 m at Oboro, 80 m at Akata — it splits the wells into a
west block (Ekene-1, -3, -5, -7 = four) and an east block (Ekene-2, -4,
-6 = three). That is exactly the split Episode 7 needs to film kriging
falling back for want of control. Small throw at the Ekene level keeps
the gridded structure and the locked volumetrics intact.

### 4.4 The consistency chain

The point of generating rather than inventing is that these agree:

- **Sw** comes from the Leverett-J drainage curve at height above the
  contact, so the logs reproduce the SCAL fixture.
- **Rw** is 35,000 ppm brine at formation temperature, 0.0785 ohm-m at
  180 °F, so the Pickett plot in Episode 2 recovers it.
- **RHOB and DT** give the acoustic impedance, so the synthetic in
  Episode 6 ties without doctoring.
- **Pore pressure** must land on the locked Pi of 3200 psia at the Ekene
  Sand — which, at 1560 m MD, is **12.02 ppg EMW**, genuinely
  overpressured. Episode 9's prognosis is therefore checkable against a
  number the reservoir side already published, and Episode 10's mud
  window follows from it.
- **The gridded TOP_SAND surface** gives ReservoirCalc the NG5 STOIIP.

### 4.5 Two surfaces, deliberately

The gridding engine masks a surface to the hull of its control points.
That is right for a map — a map should not invent structure where nobody
drilled — and useless for a seismic cube, which has to carry a reflector
across the whole survey. It also returns null at Ekene-2, which is a hull
vertex.

So the kit carries both, and they are not interchangeable:

- the **grid** is the map, and the thing whose volumetrics are locked;
- a **multiquadric RBF with a linear drift**, exact at all seven locked
  picks and defined everywhere, is the structural truth the logs and the
  seismic are generated from.

They agree at the wells, which is what lets the synthetic tie, and differ
slightly between them, which is what real seismic and well grids do.

## 6. Gates

1. **The locked numbers survive.** The generator asserts 169 oil cells
   and a 20.2818603515625 m maximum oil column from the six-well grid,
   and reports the shift when Ekene-7 to -10 are added.
2. **Round trip.** Every LAS the generator writes parses back through
   `packages/engines/engines/wells/lasParse.js` to the values written.
3. **Petrophysics recovers the plant.** Archie on the generated curves
   returns Sw within tolerance of the J-function truth, and a Pickett fit
   over the water leg returns Rw 0.0785 ohm-m.
4. **The tie ties.** The synthetic from the generated RHOB and DT
   correlates with the generated seismic at the well within one sample.
5. **SEG-Y scans clean.** Both volumes pass
   `packages/engines/engines/seismolord/segyScan.js` with the geometry
   the generator intended.
6. **Pore pressure lands on the reservoir.** Eaton with exponent 3 on the
   generated sonic returns 12.02 ppg EMW at the Ekene Sand.
7. Full jest green.

All twenty gates green 2026-09-11; the numbers they returned are in
`DemoDataset-STATUS.md`.

## 7. Distribution

`tools/demo-dataset/` generates into `dist-demo/` (git-ignored, never
committed — the same rule as `dist/`). The kit is published as a GitHub
release on the Petrolord org, versioned `ekene-demo-vN`. Text data ships
in the repo-side kit; SEG-Y and grids are release assets only.

Each episode's script gets a short "data for this episode" note naming
the files, which is the one thing the scripts are currently missing.
