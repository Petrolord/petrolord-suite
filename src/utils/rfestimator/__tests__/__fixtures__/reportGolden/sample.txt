Basis and conventions
 Item                                As used in this report

 Recovery factor                     Fraction of OOIP at stock-tank (standard) conditions, printed in percent

 In-place volume                     volumetric, from the inputs below

 Recoverable volume                  Technically recoverable volume RF x in-place; no economic limit, no development plan and no PRMS
                                     classification applied

 Pressures                           Absolute, as entered; no datum correction

 Range                               Low and high are the edges of the analog screening range of the drive named, not P90 and P10 of a distribution

 Display units                       Oilfield (area acres, thickness ft, pressure psia, Bo RB/STB, Bgi ft3/scf, volumes STB and scf, permeability md,
                                     viscosity cP)

 Uncertainty                         Not run: the estimate is deterministic


Limits of this analysis
- A screening estimate. It does not replace a reservoir simulation, a decline or material balance forecast, or a reserves study.
- Drive-mechanism analog band: a screening range for the primary drive named; the estimate is the typical value of the band.
- Recovery depends on the development plan, well count, secondary and tertiary recovery and economics; none of these is modelled.
