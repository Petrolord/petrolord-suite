# Petrophysics Studio: Industry-Standard Gap Audit

**Date:** 2026-08-23
**Method:** code-verified audit of the Suite Petrophysics Studio
(`src/pages/apps/PetrophysicsStudio/`) against professional log-analysis
practice (Techlog / IP / Geolog class workflows). Every claim below was
verified against code, not doc claims. Companion to the NextGen depth

## Headline

The foundation is genuinely strong and is NOT the problem. The
validated engine core, the registry integration and the workstation
shell are real. The gaps cluster in four bands:

1. **Trust and provenance:** results leave the app with no export or
   report, and interpretations have no named versions, so nothing is
   submission-grade or reviewable.
2. **Professional-workflow gaps:** one global parameter set, one
   implicit project, no per-zone physics, and several standard
   workflows (permeability, environmental corrections, multi-mineral)
   absent.
3. **Data and UX:** single-well viewing only, hard-error on wells
   without depth curves, no in-app import guidance.
4. **Adjacent cleanup:** one mock app still standing in the module.

## Verified baseline (the floor the owner already owns)

All verified 2026-08-23:

- **Real app, real math.** `PetrophysicsStudio.jsx` at route
  `apps/geoscience/petrophysics-studio` (`src/App.jsx:436`);
  `components/PetroWorkstation.jsx` (453 lines) orchestrates
  WellExplorer, ParameterPanel (draft-and-apply), ZoneManager with live
  net/NTG/average summaries, canvas TrackViewer (zoom/pan/crosshair,
  zone bands, tops, facies strip), CrossplotPanel (neutron-density with
  lithology lines and polygon facies tagging; Pickett with iso-Sw
  overlays and a depth-windowed fit that writes m and Rw back to
  parameters), BatchRunDialog, DigitizerDialog, registry publish and
  project save. Dev harness at `/dev/petrophysics-studio`.
- **Engine-clean.** `engine/*.js` are 2-line re-export shims into the
  vendored central subtree `packages/engines/engines/petrophysics/`
  (vsh, porosity, sw, rw, netpay, crossplot, pipeline, digitizer).
  Zero duplicate math in the app.
- **Validation-first.** Fixtures generated by an independent Python
  oracle written from primary literature
  (`tools/validation/petrophysics/oracle.py`, byte-identical
  regeneration per `packages/engines/test-data/petrophysics/README.md`);
  6 jest suites in `__tests__/` including goldens and publish.
- **Registry-integrated.** `services/registryBackend.js` reads and
  writes through `src/lib/wellsRegistry.js`: computed curves land in
  `geo_wells_logs` with `provenance.engine='petrophysics-studio'` under
  an overwrite-own-output rule (`registryBackend.js:25-27`), zones in
  `geo_wells_zones`, app state in `petro_projects` (migration
  `20260713220000`).
- **Consolidated.** The six legacy petrophysics tiles redirect here
  (`src/App.jsx:440-445`); the legacy calculation utility trees are
  deleted.

Method coverage today: Vsh (linear, Larionov tertiary and older,
Clavier, Steiber), porosity (density, Wyllie, RHG, neutron-density,
shale-corrected), Sw (Archie, Simandoux, Indonesia), Rw (Arps, SSP to
Rwe, Pickett fit), net pay with phi/Vsh/Sw cutoffs.

## Proposed waves (owner-pick; nothing approved)

Engines-first rule applies to every wave: any new math (permeability,
conditioning, multi-mineral) is implemented and oracle-gated in the
central `Petrolord/petrolord-engines` repo, subtree-pulled into both
Suite and NextGen, so the academy inherits it for future course tiers
at zero extra cost.

- **Wave A: Deliverables and provenance (A1, A3, C2, C3, D1a).** CSV +
  LAS export of computed curves, a one-page zone-summary PDF report in
  the house chart style, parameter provenance stamped on publish,
  empty-state instead of hard error, retire the mock QC tile. No new
  math. DDL: none. Effort: M. This is the wave that makes the app
  submission-grade and is the natural first pick.
- **Wave B: Interpretations and parameters (A2, B1).** Named
  interpretation versions (save-as, list, compare two side by side) and
  per-zone parameter overrides with batch runs honouring them. DDL:
  yes, `petro_projects` evolution (staging-first migration per
  conventions). Effort: M-L. Unlocks the versioned re-booking
  discipline the Expert course teaches.
- **Wave C: Permeability (B2).** Timur / Coates / Wyllie-Rose family in
  engines with an independent oracle and literature citations, then a
  perm track + zone perm averages in the UI. Effort: M. First new-math
  wave; also seeds a future NextGen course tier.
- **Wave D: Conditioning (B3, D1b).** Despike, depth-shift and bad-hole
  flagging in engines; a QC step in the workstation ahead of
  computation; optionally rebuild the QC tile as its front-end.
  Effort: M-L.
- **Wave E: Field view (C1).** Multi-well side-by-side depth panel for
  cross-well parameter QC. Effort: M.
- **Recorded, not proposed:** B4 multi-mineral solver (owner decision
  whether the product aims at that tier), B5 Bateman-Konen (opens when
  a page-referenced source is provided, per validation doctrine).
